Subject: A quiet introduction from fellow practitioners – A publishing dialogue regarding THE LIVES MEDIA

Dear Tianti Books Editorial Team,

My name is Aiden Lee. I am writing to you on behalf of THE LIVES MEDIA, an independent initiative dedicated to publishing works that foster moral clarity and spiritual awakening.

As fellow practitioners, we reach out with deep respect for the unique role Tianti Books plays in preserving and spreading the Truth. We understand that your selection process is guided by high standards and a profound sense of responsibility. It is in this spirit of transparency that we wish to introduce our work, allowing you to judge for yourselves whether it resonates with your mission.

THE LIVES MEDIA is a small, self-funded group of international practitioners. We operate independently with a focus on creating high-quality literature that speaks to the conscience of the modern public—presenting the beauty of the Fa and the gravity of the persecution through lived experience and moral reflection.

I would like to introduce three works from our current project, all of which directly engage with the persecution of Falun Gong in China through spiritual testimony rather than political rhetoric:

	- RED DUST, GOLDEN LIGHT: A journey of an intellectual seeking meaning across lifetimes, eventually finding the light of faith amidst the shadows of suppression.
	- THE RED VEIL: The first-hand testimony of a former official’s painful awakening and his discovery of the spiritual strength of a faith he once dismissed.
	- ENTERING THE WORLD: The powerful memoir of a former Red Guard who spent thirty years searching for the True Dharma, only to face the storm of persecution after finding it.

For your convenience, I have attached PDF manuscripts of these three titles to this email, as they most directly align with the mission and focus of Tianti Books.

These works are part of a broader ten-book publishing project by THE LIVES MEDIA. The remaining seven titles explore related themes from wider philosophical, historical, and spiritual perspectives. Should there be further interest, I would be honored to share additional manuscripts for your consideration at a later stage.

We fully respect Tianti Books’ vision and long-term standards. The purpose of this email is not to request immediate cooperation, but to open a channel for dialogue—should you feel that our work is aligned in spirit and worthy of consideration.

Thank you for the quiet and steadfast work you continue to do. Regardless of the outcome, we hold deep respect for Tianti Books’ role in preserving upright values.

With respect and Heshi,

Aiden Lee
