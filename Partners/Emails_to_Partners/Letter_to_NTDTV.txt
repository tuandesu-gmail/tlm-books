Subject: Collaboration Proposal: Bridging Literature and Media – Introducing THE LIVES MEDIA Book Series

Dear NTD Editorial and Media Team,

My name is Aiden Lee, and I am writing to you on behalf of THE LIVES MEDIA—an independent, self-funded publishing initiative founded by a group of international Falun Dafa practitioners.

We have long admired NTD’s steadfast commitment to restoring traditional values and providing truthful reporting to millions worldwide. As fellow practitioners, we recognize that NTD is not just a media outlet, but a vital bridge connecting modern society to the profound truths of human existence and the reality of the persecution in China.

THE LIVES MEDIA is currently developing a comprehensive 10-book project available in multiple languages. Our works focus on:

	- Spiritual Awakening: The journey of finding faith in a turbulent modern world.
	- Human Testimonies: First-hand accounts of the persecution, told through narrative prose rather than political commentary.
	- The Intersection of Science & Faith: Reflections on history, morality, and the future of humanity.
	
Key titles in our collection include:

	- THE RED VEIL: A former official’s awakening to the spiritual strength of a faith he once suppressed.
	- ENTERING THE WORLD: A former Red Guard’s 30-year search for the True Dharma.
	- BEFORE US: A contemplative look at humanity’s moral choices from a cultivation perspective.
	
Our Vision for Collaboration:
Knowing that NTD excels in multimedia storytelling, we are exploring ways to bring these powerful narratives to your global audience. We are open to various forms of collaboration, such as:
	1. Serialized Content: Adapting book chapters for online features or video segments.
	2. Author Interviews/Features: Sharing the profound stories behind these books.
	3. Media Promotion: Exploring how these works can be introduced to viewers who are seeking truth and moral clarity.
	
Attached to this email, please find the PDF manuscripts (English & Chinese) of The Red Veil and Before Us for your initial reference.

We fully respect NTD’s editorial standards and would be honored to hear your thoughts on how our content might align with your mission. Thank you for your tireless work in being a voice for the voiceless.
