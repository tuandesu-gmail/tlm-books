As a first point of introduction, I would like to share one representative work from our catalog, which I have attached to this email in PDF form:

THE RED VEIL
As told to Sophia Bell (based on interviews with a former Chinese official who defected to the United States)

This book tells the true story of Liu Siyuan, a former high-ranking Chinese official whose life was shattered when his only daughter was killed by the regime solely for her faith in Falun Gong. What follows is not a political analysis, but a painful inner awakening—from belief in the system to the recognition of a machinery built on lies and violence, and finally to an understanding of the spiritual strength he once rejected.

THE RED VEIL is an insider’s testimony, written as a human story of loss, conscience, and moral reckoning. It gives voice to countless silent victims, and bears witness to the price a society pays when faith and humanity are crushed.
