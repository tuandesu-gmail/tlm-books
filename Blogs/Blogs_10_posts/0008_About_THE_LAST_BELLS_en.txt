Book Introduction: THE LAST BELLS


Expert: Deciphering God's tear, exposing the injustice of the century, and sounding the final bell.


INTRODUCTION – DECIPHERING A SACRED TEAR

Are there sorrows of an era that can only be measured by the tears of the Divine? Amid the chaotic upheavals of the world, are there warnings issued not through sound, but through a silent compassion, manifested in omens beyond human understanding?

It is from these haunting questions that the book "THE LAST BELLS" was born. The author, Taylor Reed, with the sharp foundation of an investigative journalist and the depth of a spiritual practitioner, leads us on a unique and breathtaking journey. This journey begins not in a historical archive or a laboratory, but with a sacred and haunting moment: the image of a pure white porcelain statue of the Virgin Mary, from whose eye a dark teardrop had streamed down her cheek.

In a world screaming in chaos, that silent tear became the loudest sound the author had ever heard. It posed a wordless question, an indescribable sorrow: "Why must She weep?". That question haunted Taylor Reed, compelling her to use everything she had—the mind of a journalist, the caution of a researcher, and the restless heart of a believer—to embark on an investigation unlike any other: a journey to find the cause of a sacred tear.


DIAGNOSING THE "SICKNESS OF THE AGE"

The book does not rush to find answers in mysterious prophecies. Instead, the author begins with a systematic "diagnosis" of our modern civilization. With a sharp and contemplative prose, Taylor Reed uncovers four major "case files," exposing the hollowing and decay occurring in every aspect of human life.

	Case File #1: The Corrosion of the Creative Soul (Culture & Arts): The author presents a scathing analysis of contemporary art's decline. From global music products engineered to be addictive rather than inspiring, with dehumanizing choreography and empty lyrics ; to modern painting, where a banana taped to a wall is sold for hundreds of thousands of dollars and hailed as "peak thought". Taylor Reed argues that art, once a torch to illuminate the soul, has now become a tool for the legal corrosion of conscience, dragging humanity into darkness under the guise of "creativity".

	Case File #2: The Compromise of the Intellect (Media & Social Media): Once the "fourth estate," journalism today, according to the author, has abandoned its role of protecting the truth to chase "clicks" and serve backroom interests. Meanwhile, social media platforms like TikTok and YouTube Shorts are restructuring the brains of an entire generation, creating a society that is "lazy to read," afraid of deep thought, and easily led by fleeting emotions. Truth requires time to be understood, while a lie needs only seconds to provoke outrage. In this battle, wisdom itself is facing extinction.

	Case File #3: The Chaos of Foundational Values (Ethics & Society): The family, the primary foundation for learning how to be human, is being undermined by "modern" concepts where roles and gender become optional choices. When morality is detached from family and school, where do children learn to be human? Social ills, from drugs and prostitution to fraud, are not only increasing but are being disguised as "diverse lifestyles". In particular, extreme ideas about "gender freedom" and same-sex marriage are upending natural laws and traditional ethics, creating a chaos of foundational values upon which no society can stand.

	Case File #4: The Hollowing of Power and Faith (Politics & Religion): Whether in Western democracies or Eastern autocracies, modern politics, the author argues, has lost its moral foundation. One side features a game of image-maintenance dominated by media and money; the other features absolute control to protect the power of a single party. In parallel, religion, once a spiritual anchor, is being commercialized. God's house has been turned into a "marketplace", where faith becomes mere decoration for events and is even exploited to incite war. When power is hollowed out and faith is distorted, the ship of civilization begins to sink.


DECODING THE OMENS – WHEN HEAVEN AND EARTH SPEAK

After exposing the "symptoms" of a world gravely ill, Taylor Reed begins to connect them to the "anomalous phenomena" occurring everywhere. Not just the weeping sacred statues, but also rivers suddenly turning blood-red, illogical snowfalls during hot summers, and unseasonal hailstorms sowing terror.

The key to deciphering these omens comes from an ancient Chinese classic: "Snow in June". The story of the immense injustice suffered by Dou E, which moved Heaven and Earth to respond by making it snow in midsummer, leads the author to a realization: today's anomalous phenomena are not random acts of nature, but the "cries" of Heaven and Earth in the face of a great injustice, a crime so immense it is being hidden in our time.

What injustice is great enough to make the gods weep and Heaven send snow in summer? The author's investigation leads to a shocking conclusion: it is the brutal and systematic persecution of Falun Gong practitioners in China since 1999. The pinnacle of this crime, an "unprecedented evil on this planet," is the forced live organ harvesting from peaceful cultivators, turning them into a "live organ bank" to fuel a lucrative transplant industry. The remaining bodies may even be plastinated and displayed in exhibitions in the name of science and art. This is the greatest injustice of our time, a crime against humanity hidden behind the "red veil" of propaganda and the world's silence.


THE CONVERGENCE OF PROPHECY AND THE CALL TO CHOOSE

Having identified the "great injustice" of the age, Taylor Reed proceeds to connect it with ancient prophecies from both East and West. From Nostradamus's mysterious verses about a "King of Terror" appearing in "July 1999," to the prophecies of Liu Bowen and Shao Yong about a final tribulation period that reveal the salvific role of the three words "Truthfulness-Compassion-Forbearance". All seem to converge, pointing to one time, one event, and one path of choice for humanity.

The book asserts that we are living in a special "transitional period," a time when ancient prophecies appear to be fulfilling with greater clarity than ever before, and humanity stands before a "great judgment" based on each individual's choice between Good and Evil . The "Last Bells" are not bells of doom, but bells to awaken conscience, an urgent call to return to foundational moral values, to courageously stand on the side of truth and justice before it is too late.

Taylor Reed's journey ends with her return to the weeping statue of the Virgin Mary. But this time, it is not with questions, but with a profound empathy. Her own tears merge with the sacred tear, not out of despair, but out of an infinite compassion and an awakening from within. This book, therefore, is not just an investigation but a prayer, a tribute, and a fervent hope that, no matter how thick the darkness, the light of conscience and compassion will never be extinguished.
