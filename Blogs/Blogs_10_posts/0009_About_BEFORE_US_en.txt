Book Introduction: BEFORE US

Expert: A rule-breaking chronicle, where 200 million years of history are "seen" directly through the third eye of a cultivator.


INTRODUCTION – WHEN HISTORY IS NO LONGER AN EXCAVATION

Human history has, until now, been pieced together from fragments—silent ruins, faded texts, and the ceaseless speculation of archaeologists. But what if there were another way to know the past? Not by excavating what is dead, but by directly "witnessing" what once lived?

"BEFORE US" is such a publishing event, a chronicle that breaks all the rules. It is not based on physical evidence or academic theories, but is recorded entirely from the account of a spiritual cultivator whose third eye has been opened. With the ability to see through other dimensions of space and time, the narrator does not "speculate" about history, but "sees" it as a vivid film spanning 200 million years. Journalist Jack Voss, in his role as a faithful scribe, brings us a picture of history previously unknown, an "echo" that is no longer vague but astonishingly clear.


A CHRONICLE OF GODS, HUMANS, AND DEMONS

Prepare to enter a past that is grander and more tragic than any myth. This is no longer a story of dynasties or conventional wars. This is the history of Earth itself and the beings who once ruled it in different civilizational cycles:

	The Lost Eras: The book reveals in detail magnificent eras that once existed: The Age of Giants, where dinosaurs were used as mounts; civilizations at the bottom of the deep sea; and even a cycle of civilization in which the Moon was crafted as a giant spaceship for an unimaginable purpose.

	Cosmic Wars and the Origin of Humanity: Earth's history is not separate from the cosmos. The work exposes the wars between the forces of Gods and Demons in different dimensions, and the intervention of extraterrestrial races in humanity's development. Humans did not evolve from apes, but have a higher, more sacred origin, and the human body is a perfect creation coveted by many forces in the universe.

	The Plot for the Human Body: The work reveals sophisticated and long-standing conspiracies by certain extraterrestrial forces to possess or alter the human body. Why is the human body so precious? And what role do modern technologies like AI or genetic engineering play in this plan?


CONCLUSION – REDISCOVERING HUMANITY'S TRUE SELF

"BEFORE US" is not merely an alternative history book. It is a revelation, a call to radically re-examine the nature of who we are. By exposing a hidden past, the book forces us to confront the most fundamental questions: Who are we really? Why are we here? And why is Earth the central stage for such a vast and complex cosmic drama?

This is a book for courageous souls, for those who are not afraid to have their worldview challenged to its very core. Let us listen to the direct "echoes" from the currents of time, to understand our true place in the universe, and to find the path back to the true origin of life.
